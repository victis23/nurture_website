Nurtur: AI Baby Assistant — Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: May 23, 2026

1 Overview

You are always in control of your data. You can delete your Nurtur account and all of the personal information associated with it at any time — either directly inside the app or by sending us a request.

1.1 This page explains exactly how to delete your data and what happens after you do. It complements Section 7 (Data Retention) and Section 8 (Your Rights and Choices) of our Privacy Policy.

1.2 Nurtur signs you in with Apple. We do not use Facebook Login, so there is no Facebook account or Facebook profile data linked to your Nurtur account. For information about advertising data that Meta may collect through the Meta Pixel on this website, see Section 5 below.

2 Option A — Delete Your Account in the App

2.1 The fastest way to delete your data is from inside the app:

2.2 Open Nurtur, go to Settings, and tap Delete Account. You will be asked to confirm.

2.3 Once you confirm, your account is deactivated immediately and your data is queued for permanent removal as described in Section 4.

2.4 This action is permanent and cannot be undone. If you want a copy of your data first, you can export it from the Settings screen before deleting.

3 Option B — Request Deletion by Email

3.1 If you can no longer access the app, you can ask us to delete your data on your behalf.

3.2 Email us at support@trynurtur.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line "Data Deletion Request."

3.3 So we can locate and verify your account, please include the name or email you used to sign in with Apple. We may ask you to confirm a small additional detail to make sure we are deleting the right account.

3.4 We will confirm receipt and complete your request within 30 days, and sooner where reasonably possible.

4 What We Delete and When

4.1 When you delete your account, we permanently remove the data tied to it, including:

4.2 Your account identifier and any name or email you shared through Sign in with Apple; your baby and care data (profiles, feeds, sleep sessions, diaper changes, mood, and other logged events); and your AI questions, the responses, and the parsed insights saved to your account.

4.3 We remove this data from our active systems within 30 days of your request. Residual copies in encrypted backups may persist for a short additional period before they are overwritten on our normal backup cycle.

4.4 We may retain a minimal amount of information where we are legally required to do so (for example, records needed to comply with tax, accounting, or fraud-prevention obligations). Such information is kept only as long as the law requires and is not used for any other purpose.

4.5 Your Apple subscription is managed by Apple. Deleting your Nurtur account does not cancel an active subscription — you can manage or cancel it in your device's Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

5 Facebook / Meta Advertising Data

5.1 This website uses the Meta Pixel to measure the performance of our advertising. The pixel may record that you visited a page on this site. It is not connected to your in-app baby or care data, and we do not use Facebook Login.

5.2 Because this data is collected and held by Meta under their own policies, you control it through your Meta account rather than through Nurtur:

5.3 In your Facebook or Instagram settings, you can review and clear Off-Facebook activity, adjust your Ad preferences, and disconnect future activity from your account.

5.4 You can also limit ad tracking at the device or browser level — for example, through your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking controls.

5.5 If you would like our help understanding what the pixel collects, email us at support@trynurtur.com and we will do our best to assist.

6 Contact Us

6.1 If you have any questions about deleting your data, or you need help completing a request, contact us using the information below.
